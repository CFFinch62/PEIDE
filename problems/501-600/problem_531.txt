Problem 531: Chinese Leftovers

<p>
Let $g(a, n, b, m)$ be the smallest non-negative solution $x$ to the system:<br>
$x = a \bmod n$<br>
$x = b \bmod m$<br>
if such a solution exists, otherwise $0$.
</p>
<p>
E.g. $g(2,4,4,6)=10$, but $g(3,4,4,6)=0$.
</p>
<p>
Let $\phi(n)$ be Euler's totient function.
</p>
<p>
Let $f(n,m)=g(\phi(n),n,\phi(m),m)$
</p>
<p>
Find $\sum f(n,m)$ for $1000000 \le n \lt m \lt 1005000$.
</p>

Hint: 
1. Break down the problem into smaller steps
2. Consider mathematical properties that might simplify the solution
3. Think about efficient algorithms to solve this type of problem
