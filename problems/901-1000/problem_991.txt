Problem 991: Fruit Salad

<p>Find the sum of $\unicode{x1F34E} + \unicode{x1F34C} + \unicode{x1F34D}$ for all triples of positive integers $\unicode{x1F34E}, \unicode{x1F34C}, \unicode{x1F34D}$ which satisfy</p>

$$\frac {\unicode{x1F34E}} {\unicode{x1F34C} + \unicode{x1F34D}} +
\frac {\unicode{x1F34C}} {\unicode{x1F34D} + \unicode{x1F34E}} +
\frac {\unicode{x1F34D}} {\unicode{x1F34E} + \unicode{x1F34D}} = 4$$

<p>and $\unicode{x1F34E} + \unicode{x1F34C} + \unicode{x1F34D} \le 10^7$.</p>
