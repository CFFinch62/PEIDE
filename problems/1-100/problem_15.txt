Problem 15: Lattice Paths

<p>Starting in the top left corner of a $2 \times 2$ grid, and only being able to move to the right and down, there are exactly $6$ routes to the bottom right corner.</p>
<div class="center">
<img src="resources/images/0015.png" class="dark_img" alt=""></div>
<p>How many such routes are there through a $20 \times 20$ grid?</p>

Hint:
1. This is a combinatorics problem
2. For an n×n grid, the number of paths is the central binomial coefficient C(2n, n)
3. Consider using dynamic programming or mathematical formula
4. Be careful with large numbers and integer overflow
