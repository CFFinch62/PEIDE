Problem 830: Binomials and Powers

<p>
Let $\displaystyle S(n)=\sum\limits_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}k^n$.</p>

<p>
You are given, $S(10)=142469423360$.</p>

<p>
Find $S(10^{18})$. Submit your answer modulo $83^3 89^3 97^3$.</p>
